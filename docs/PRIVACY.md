# Tomato Life Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

Tomato Life does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or user data.

- The Chrome extension reads only the local system clock to decide whether it is work time or rest time.
- It makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and has no accounts.
- Its two permissions stay on your device: `alarms` updates the toolbar badge once a minute, and `offscreen` plays the chime sound.
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, because nothing is collected.

Questions: <https://github.com/snomiao/tomato-life/issues>
